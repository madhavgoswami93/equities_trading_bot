Swingbot Architecture

One strategy file drives backtesting, paper trading and live trading through the same engine. You need one broker API, Kite or Groww, for orders. Long price history is backfilled once from free Yahoo data into a local DuckDB database, and everything else stays on your machine.

Three modes, one engine

Backtest history

Starts from empty state and runs the kernel across the full price history. Writes stats, trades and equity to runs/<name>/backtest/.

Paper daily

Loads yesterday's book.json and runs the same kernel over the new bars only. A test confirms it reproduces the backtest trade for trade.

Live Kite

Real Kite fills replace the simulated ones, then the same close-of-day decision runs. Dry run until dry_run = false and --send.

A live trading day (IST)

08:45
Kite login

Kite only: swingbot kite-login stores today's access token in .secrets/. Groww's TOTP login needs no daily step.

09:15
Market open

Yesterday's AMO limit buys and market sells execute at the open on NSE.

09:16
Open phase

live --phase open --send sends Groww's queued buys and sells (Groww has no after-market orders), syncs fills and places GTT stops on new positions.

15:30
Market close

Unfilled entry orders expire.

15:45
Close phase

live --send refuses to run on stale data, fetches the day's candles into the database, syncs exits, runs the engine on today's close, trails stops upward and sends tomorrow's orders (Kite as after-market orders, Groww queued for the open).

16:15
Paper step

paper advances the simulated book on the same data for comparison.
